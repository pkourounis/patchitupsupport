## How much *should* you spend? (the guardrail)

Two simple rules to keep spend disciplined regardless of which option you pick:

1. **Cost-per-sold-estimate ceiling:** keep blended paid cost per sold estimate **under ~15% of your average job value.** (Your median sold estimate in this data was ~$549; plug in your real average ticket and margin to set the exact ceiling.)
2. **Marketing as % of revenue:** home-services franchises typically run **8–12% of revenue** on lead-gen/marketing. Set your monthly budget to land in that band once you know your booked revenue.

If a channel can't beat the ceiling after the fixes below, cut it.

## Do these first — they cost nothing and raise every channel's return

1. **15-minute response rule.** In your own data, Thumbtack leads that booked were answered in ~144 min; leads that died sat ~752 min (5× slower). Speed is free conversion.
2. **Filter Angi & Thumbtack** by ZIP (stop paying for SC/out-of-area) and by category (drop low-value job types). Dispute spam/out-of-area for refunds — Thumbtack already refunded 6.
3. **Turn on the referral flywheel.** Referrals and power partners closed at 67–100%. A simple "ask + small reward" program and a few HVAC/restoration partners will out-earn any paid channel per dollar.
4. **Pull Yelp / GLSA / Google PPC billing** before renewing — they produced almost nothing (combined 5 booked / 1 won across the whole book).
